# DemoPay Company Knowledge Base

## Virtual Accounts

Customers receive a virtual account after completing KYC verification.

## Transfer Limits

The maximum daily transfer limit is ₦500,000.

## Failed Transfers

Failed transfers are automatically reversed within 24 hours.

If the reversal has not happened after 24 hours, the customer should contact support.

## KYC Requirements

Customers need a valid government-issued identification document and BVN to complete verification.

## International Transfers

International transfers are processed through supported payment partners.

Processing time depends on the destination country and payment partner.
